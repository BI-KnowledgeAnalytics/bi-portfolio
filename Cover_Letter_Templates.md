## Upwork-specific tips
- First 2 lines are everything — Upwork shows only ~2 lines before "more."
  Open with a direct result, not "I am a hardworking..."
- Always fill in [$$/hr] — proposals with a clear rate get more replies.
- Mirror their exact keywords (e.g. "DAX", "Fabric") to match search filters.
- One portfolio link max per proposal.
- Change 1–2 lines per job — a tailored opening beats a 100% generic blurb.
