Typical engagement

From raw tables to a trustworthy dashboard in about three weeks.

  1. Week 1 Source & structure

    We review the data, agree on the grain, and load the model you can build on.

  2. Week 2 Measures & first views

    Key metrics are written and every number checked against the source.

  3. Week 3 Design & ship

    Pages finished, documented, tested — then handed over with training.
